Who Stole My Takeout?

Who Stole My Takeout? (the “Game”) is an offline runner. We handle as little of your information as possible: the Game does not collect, upload or share any personal data, and it contains no advertising, analytics or third-party SDKs.

What is stored on your device

  • Settings: brightness, volume, language.
  • Progress: high score, achievement unlocks, total milk teas, total obstacles smashed, and whether the optional in-app purchase is unlocked.
  • All of this lives in local storage on your device. Deleting the app removes it, and we have no way to access it.

Game Center

  • If you are signed in to Game Center, the Game submits your run score and chase duration to Apple leaderboards and reports achievement progress to Apple.
  • The nickname shown on the leaderboard comes from your Game Center profile, and other players can see that nickname and your score.
  • Apple processes this data under Apple's Privacy Policy. You can sign out of Game Center at any time in Settings to stop the uploads.

In-app purchase

  • The “Extra Life” purchase is handled by the Apple App Store. We never see your payment details, name or address.
  • Apple verifies the receipt; the Game only stores a local flag for whether it is unlocked.

Sharing

  • When you share your result, the card is rendered on your device and handed to the system share sheet, so you choose where it goes. We do not upload it.

Children

  • The Game has no chat, no user-generated content uploads and no personal data collection, and is suitable for all ages.

Your rights

  • Because we collect no personal data and run no servers, there is no account or cloud data to delete. Uninstalling the app clears everything stored locally.

Changes

  • If this policy changes, we will post the new version on this page and update the effective date.

Contact

  • Questions about this policy? Reach us through the support page: xingran0720@gmail.com.

Game Center

  • Game Center에 로그인한 경우, 본 게임은 한 판의 점수와 추격 시간을 Apple 리더보드에 제출하고 업적 진행도를 Apple에 보고합니다.

  • 이 데이터는 Apple이 처리하며 Apple 개인정보 보호정책이 적용됩니다. 설정에서 Game Center 로그아웃하면 업로드가 중단됩니다.

  • 영수증은 Apple이 검증하며, 본 게임은 “해제 여부”만 기기에 기록합니다.

공유 기능

  • 결과를 공유할 때 카드 이미지는 기기에서 생성되어 시스템 공유 시트로 전달됩니다. 어디로 보낼지는 이용자가 정하며, 저희는 업로드하지 않습니다.
